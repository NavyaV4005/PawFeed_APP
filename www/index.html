About Us

At PawFeed, we believe every pet deserves the best care possible. Founded in 2026, we set out to build the most comprehensive, AI-driven platform for pet parents worldwide.

Whether you're tracking daily water intake, managing complex medical records, or looking for community-approved homemade recipes, PawFeed is designed to make pet parenting joyful and stress-free.

🐾 PawFeed

Features

  • Smart Care Planner: Never miss a walk, feed, or pill.
  • Homemade Food Studio: Vet-approved recipes with built-in calculators.
  • Medical Vault: Store PDFs of lab results and vaccination history securely.
  • PawFeed AI: 24/7 instant advice for your pet's wellness.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PawFeed free?

Yes! The core features are completely free. We offer premium AI features for advanced analytics.

Can I share my account with my partner?

Yes, use our Household Sharing feature in Settings to link accounts securely.
